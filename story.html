🌸 Hamari Kahani — Shayari Ke Andaaz Mein 🌸

▶️❤️
YouTube ki ek live/class si mehfil thi, aur bheed mein ek chhoti si mulaqat hui,
Tab kya pata tha ek simple sa hello, kal meri favourite kahani banegi wahi. 🥹
Pehle baatein thi seedhi-si, kabhi “bhai”, kabhi “sister” ka naam,
Phir hasi-mazaak aur live chat ne bana diya apna sa ek chhota jahaan. 😂❤️
YouTube se Telegram ka safar hua, messages ka silsila chala,
Ek notification se smile aayi, aur har din dil thoda aur khila. 📱💗
Pehle bas reply hota tha, phir reply ka intezaar hone laga,
Tum online dikho toh smile, na dikho toh phone baar-baar khulne laga. 😂
Telegram se WhatsApp, WhatsApp se Instagram ka rang,
Snapchat ne add kiye aur bhi funny moments ke sang. ✨
Platform badalte gaye, par ek cheez wahi rahi—
Har naye chat ke saath tumhari importance aur badhti gayi. ❤️
Kabhi late reply par “busy hogi” dil ko samjhaya,
Kabhi “online ho, reply kyun nahi?” wala sawaal khud hi banaya. 😂😭
Kabhi jealousy ka chhota sa chapter, kabhi “kuch nahi” ka dialogue,
Aur andar hi andar chal raha tha poora investigation log. 😂❤️
Kabhi bina wajah fight, kabhi bina wajah hasi,
Kabhi roothna, kabhi manaana — har memory thi apni si. 🥹
Tumhara “hmm”, tumhara “jao”, tumhara gussa bhi yaad reh gaya,
Har chhoti nok-jhok ke baad dil aur paas aa gaya. ❤️
Dheere-dheere friendship ne ek naya naam paaya,
Jo feeling kal tak samajh nahi aayi, usne aaj dil ko samjhaya.
Do alag log, do alag duniya, phir bhi ek connection khaas,
Aur 11 mahino mein lagne laga — jaise ban gaya apna ek jahaan paas. 🌙❤️
Long-distance ne raaste door kiye, kahani ko nahi,
Doori ne bas yeh sikhaya ki care kam hoti nahi. 🌍❤️
Calls, chats, memories aur chhoti-chhoti baatein saath chalti rahi,
Aur har naye din ke saath “hum” ki kahani likhti rahi.
11 September phir se aaya, toh dil ne bas itna kaha—
“Achha hua us din hum mile the,” warna yeh kahani milti kahan? 🥹❤️
Na story perfect thi, na har chapter easy tha,
Par shayad isi liye har moment itna real aur beautiful tha.
Aaj jo pages likhe hain, kal aur pages judenge,
Naye jokes, naye moments, naye memories phir se banenge. ✨
Kahani ka ending abhi likha hi kahan hai,
Kyuki hamari story ka sabse beautiful part — abhi aage likhna baaki hai. ❤️
