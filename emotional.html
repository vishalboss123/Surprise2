One Last Feeling

Paggli… ❤️

Paggli, shayad main perfect nahi hoon…
shayad meri kuch baatein tumhe hurt karti hain,
shayad main har baar apne dil ki baat sahi tarah keh nahi pata…

Lekin ek baat sach hai—
jo feelings tumhare liye hain, woh jhooth nahi hain. ❤️

11 September ko jab hum mile the,
tab mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek simple si mulaqat
meri life ki itni beautiful story ban jayegi…

Main tumhe har cheez nahi bata pata,
har feeling explain nahi kar pata,
kyunki kabhi-kabhi ghar ke pressure aur situations
mujhe apne emotions andar rakhne par majboor kar dete hain.

Agar kabhi tumhe laga ho ki main badal gaya hoon,
toh bas itna samajhna—
feelings badli nahi hain, bas situations difficult hui hain. ❤️‍🩹

Main maanta hoon, mujhse mistakes hoti hain.
Main bura insaan nahi banna chahta,
main bas ek aisa insaan hoon jo tumhe sach mein care karta hai
aur har din khud ko better karna seekh raha hai.

Tum meri story ka koi temporary chapter nahi ho…
tum woh chapter ho jise main khatam nahi karna chahta. ❤️

11 September hamari beginning thi…
aur aaj bhi main chahta hoon ki
hamari story ke aage ke pages
hum dono milkar likhein. 🥹❤️

Agar meri kisi baat se kabhi tum hurt hui ho,
toh sorry…
aur agar kabhi meri khamoshi ne tumhe confuse kiya ho,
toh uske peeche pyaar ki kami nahi thi.
Bas main har baar apne dil ki baat keh nahi pata tha. ❤️‍🩹

Kabhi-kabhi words kam pad jaate hain… 🥹❤️

Shayad main har baar tumhe woh sab nahi bata pata jo mere dil mein hota hai…
lekin iska matlab ye kabhi nahi tha ki tum mere liye important nahi ho.

Kuch feelings aisi hoti hain jinke liye perfect words milna mushkil hota hai.
Bas ek naam yaad aata hai…
aur woh naam tumhara hai. ❤️

Hamari journey mein sab kuch easy nahi raha.
Kabhi hasi thi, kabhi chhoti si narazgi,
kabhi misunderstanding, kabhi silence…
lekin har phase ne hume kuch na kuch sikhaya.

Maine tumse ye seekha ki kisi ko care karna sirf words bolna nahi hota.
Kabhi samajhna hota hai, kabhi patiently sunna hota hai,
aur kabhi apni mistake accept karke better banna hota hai.

Main ye nahi keh raha ki future mein kabhi mistake nahi hogi…
lekin main itna zaroor chahta hoon ki har mistake ke baad
main pehle se thoda better banun. ❤️

Tumhare saath jo memories bani hain,
woh mere liye sirf chats ya screenshots nahi hain.
Woh meri life ke woh moments hain jinhe main smile ke saath yaad karta hoon. 🥹❤️

YouTube par ek simple si mulaqat se lekar
Telegram ki endless chats tak,
WhatsApp ki conversations, Instagram ki memories,
aur Snapchat ke funny moments tak…
pata hi nahi chala kab tum itni important ban gayi.

Aur shayad isi ko toh kahani kehte hain… ❤️

Ek aisi kahani jiska beginning hume yaad hai,
jiske beech mein hazaaron memories hain,
aur jiska future abhi bhi blank pages se bhara hua hai.

Un pages par abhi bahut kuch likhna baaki hai…
naye jokes, naye moments, naye memories,
aur bahut saari hasi. ❤️✨

Aaj jab 11 September ko main peeche dekhta hoon,
toh ek hi thought baar-baar aata hai—

Achha hua us din hum mile the. 🥹❤️

Agar woh ek chhoti si mulaqat nahi hoti,
toh shayad ye beautiful journey bhi nahi hoti.
Na itni memories hoti,
na itne moments,
aur na hamari ye story. ❤️

Isliye 11 September mere liye sirf calendar ki ek date nahi hai.
Ye woh date hai jise dekhkar
mujhe hamari beginning yaad aati hai.

Aur agar tum kabhi socho ki tum meri story mein kitni important ho…
toh bas hamari purani memories ko yaad kar lena.
Shayad unmein hi tumhe answer mil jayega. ❤️‍🩹

Main bas itna chahta hoon ki hum apni story ko perfect banane ke pressure mein na rahein.
Bas real rahein, ek-doosre ko samajhne ki koshish karein,
aur jo beautiful moments milen unhe dil se enjoy karein. ❤️

Kyunki perfect story se zyada beautiful woh story hoti hai jisme do log mistakes ke baad bhi
ek-doosre ko samajhna seekhte hain.

Hamari story ka next page… abhi likhna baaki hai. 🥹❤️

Aur ab…
main tumhe ek last cheez dikhana chahta hoon…

Shayad ye page khatam ho raha hai,
lekin hamari story nahi.
Kyunki kuch stories ka ending nahi hota…
unke bas naye chapters start hote hain. ❤️✨

Toh jo kuch bhi peeche reh gaya,
usse ek beautiful memory ki tarah rakhenge.
Aur jo aage aayega,
usse milkar ek nayi memory banayenge. 🫶🏻❤️
