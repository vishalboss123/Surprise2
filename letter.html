💌 A Letter For You

“11 September — the day my favourite story began.” ❤️

11 September… Ek normal si date nahi hai, Ye woh din hai jab meri zindagi ki Sabse pyaari kahani shuru hui thi। ❤️

Na mujhe pata tha tum meri itni khaas banogi, Na tumhe pata tha main tumhara itna pagal banunga… Bas YouTube par ek chhoti si mulaqat hui, Aur pata hi nahi chala Kab woh mulaqat ek khoobsurat rishta ban gayi। 🥹❤️

YouTube se Telegram tak, Telegram se WhatsApp tak, WhatsApp se Instagram, Aur Instagram se Snapchat tak… Har platform badalta gaya, Lekin ek cheez kabhi nahi badli— Tumhare liye meri feelings। ❤️

Pehle tumhara message aata tha Toh bas reply kar deta tha, Phir tumhara message aane ka wait karne laga… Pehle online dekhta tha toh normal lagta tha, Phir tum online na dikho Toh phone baar-baar check karne laga। 😂❤️

Kabhi tum late reply karti, Toh mera dimaag kehta— “Busy hogi…” Aur mera dil kehta— “Online aake mujhe reply kyun nahi kar rahi?” 😂😭❤️

Aur jab koi tumhe message karta tha na, Bas phir meri jealousy activate! 😂 Main kuch bolta nahi tha, Bas andar hi andar “Ye kaun hai bhai?” wala mode on ho jaata tha। 😂❤️

Tum poochti— “Kya hua?”
Aur main— “Kuch bhi toh nahi।” 😌
Lekin tumhe kya pata, Us “kuch nahi” ke andar Pura investigation department chal raha hota tha। 😂❤️

Kabhi hum bina wajah ladte, Kabhi bina wajah haste, Kabhi ek-doosre ko pagal bolte, Aur kabhi itna pyaar karte Ki woh chhoti si fight bhi Kuch hi der mein cute memory ban jaati। 🥹❤️

Tumhara gussa bhi cute, Tumhara attitude bhi cute, Tumhara “hmm” bhi cute, Aur tumhara woh “jao” bolna bhi cute… Bas problem ye hai ki Tum jo bhi karti ho, Mujhe tum aur zyada cute lagti ho। 😂❤️

11 months… Log bolenge bas 11 months, Lekin mujhe toh lagta hai Jaise 11 mahino mein Humne ek poori duniya bana li। ❤️

Hazaaron messages, Hundreds of calls, Chhoti-chhoti fights, Random jokes, Late replies, Jealousy ke moments, Cute manaana, Aur woh endless conversations… Sab kuch milakar Hamari ek aisi story ban gayi Jise main baar-baar padhna chahta hoon। 🥹❤️

Kabhi tum mujhe chidhati ho, Kabhi main tumhe chidhata hoon, Kabhi tum gussa hoti ho, Aur phir main tumhe manaata hoon… Sach kahun toh Mujhe ye sab bhi pyaar hi lagta hai। ❤️

Kyunki mohabbat sirf “I love you” bolne ka naam nahi, Mohabbat toh woh hai Jahan kisi ke “good morning” se smile aaye, Uske “good night” se din complete ho, Aur uske bina kuch der bhi Thoda sa ajeeb lage। ❤️‍🩹

Tum meri favourite notification ho, Favourite chat ho, Favourite person ho… Aur honestly, Kabhi-kabhi favourite headache bhi ho। 😂❤️
Par ye headache bhi Main lifetime jhel sakta hoon। 😂❤️

YouTube ne hume milaya, Telegram ne hume baat karna sikhaya, WhatsApp ne hume aur kareeb kiya, Instagram ne memories di, Snapchat ne un memories ko Aur funny bana diya… Aur in sab ke beech hamara pyaar har din thoda aur strong hota gaya। ❤️

Aaj 11 September ko Jab main peeche dekhta hoon, Toh bas ek hi thought aata hai—
“Achha hua us din hum mile the।” 🥹❤️

Warna mujhe kaise milti Meri ye pagal si ladki? 😂❤️
Kaise milta Mujhe mera favourite insaan?
Kaise banti Itni saari beautiful memories?
Aur kaise milta Mujhe woh person Jiske saath main Bina kisi reason ke bhi Hass sakta hoon। ❤️

Meri jaan, Hamari kahani perfect nahi hai… Aur shayad isi wajah se Itni beautiful hai।
Hum ladte hain, Phir haste hain। Hum roothte hain, Phir manaate hain। Kabhi misunderstanding hoti hai, Phir ek-doosre ko samajhte hain।
Aur har baar Ek baat prove hoti hai—
Hum dono alag ho sakte hain, Par ek-doosre ke bina Hamari story incomplete hai। ❤️🥹

Aaj main tumse Kuch bada promise nahi karunga…
Bas itna kahunga—
Jab tak tum mere saath ho, Main tumhare saath Har chhoti khushi ko celebrate karunga। ❤️

Tum hasti rehna, Main tumhe hasata rahunga।
Tum gussa karna, Main tumhe manaata rahunga।
Tum mujhe pagal kehna, Main tumhe apni pagal kehkar Aur zyada pyaar karta rahunga। 😂❤️

Aur haan…
11 September sirf hamari Meet Anniversary nahi, Ye meri favourite date hai।
Kyunki isi din Meri life mein Ek aisi ladki aayi thi Jo dheere-dheere Meri favourite person ban gayi। ❤️

Happy Meet Anniversary, meri jaan. 🥹❤️

YouTube se shuru hui kahani ko Aaj 11 mahine ho gaye…
Aur sabse beautiful baat?
Kahani abhi khatam nahi hui… Kyunki hum abhi bhi saath hain। ❤️‍🩹

Aaj bhi tum meri ho, Aaj bhi main tumhara hoon, Aaj bhi hamari chat mein Wahi pyaar hai, Wahi hasi hai, Wahi pagalpan hai…
Bas ab memories ke saath aur bhi naye moments banane hain। ❤️

Love you meri jaan. ❤️ Happy Meet Anniversary to us. 🫶🏻🥹 11 September — The day I met you, and the day my favourite story began. ❤️✨
