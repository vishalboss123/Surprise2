🌙 Ek Lambi Shayari — Hamari Kahani Ke Naam 🌙

“Ek mulaqat thi chhoti si, par kahani bahut lambi nikli…” ❤️
Ek YouTube ki duniya thi, jahan hazaaron log the saath,
Unhi bheed mein mili tum, aur badal gaya mera har ek din-raat. 🥹❤️
Pehle naam se zyada kuch tha hi nahi, phir baaton ne rishta bana diya,
“Bhai-sister” ke mazaak se shuru karke dil ne kuch aur hi suna diya। 😂
Telegram ke messages ne faasle kam kiye,
WhatsApp ki chats ne raaton ko baatein di,
Instagram ne memories sambhali, Snapchat ne hasi di,
Aur har platform ne hamari kahani mein ek nayi line likh di। 💗
Kabhi reply late hua toh dil ne sawaal kiya,
Kabhi “kuch nahi” kehkar bhi andar se kamaal kiya। 😂
Kabhi chhoti si jealousy, kabhi chhoti si fight,
Phir ek cute sa manaana, aur sab kuch again alright। ❤️
Tumhara “hmm” bhi ek chapter, tumhara “jao” bhi ek baat,
Tumhari hasi ek memory, tumhari care ek saugaat। 🌸
Aur dheere-dheere friendship se feelings ka safar hua,
Pata hi nahi chala kab “main” se zyada “hum” ka zikr hua। 🥹
11 mahine mein hazaaron messages ka shehar basa,
Kabhi jokes, kabhi silence, kabhi gussa, kabhi pyaar hasa।
Dooriyan aayi toh bhi kahani rukhi nahi,
Kyunki asli connection distance se kam hota nahi। 🌍❤️
11 September aaya toh dil ne ek baat kahi—
“Achha hua us din hum mile,” warna yeh kahani milti nahi।
Perfect kuch bhi nahi tha, phir bhi sab kuch khaas raha,
Har imperfect moment mein hamara apna ehsaas raha। ✨
Kal ke pages abhi khaali hain, unmein naye moments likhenge,
Nayi hasi, naye jokes, nayi memories phir se milenge।
Aaj jo story yahan hai, woh ending nahi — ek naya chapter hai,
Kyuki hamari kahani ka agla page abhi bhi open hai। 📖❤️
YouTube se shuru hui kahani,
Telegram se bani pehchaan,
WhatsApp se badhi baatein,
Instagram se judi yaadein,
Snapchat se aayi hasi,
Aur sabse upar — hamari story ki ek khoobsurat si nishani। ❤️🫶🏻
